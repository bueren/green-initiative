Who We Are

Green Initiative is an international NGO dedicated to supporting sustainable development and climate resilience through partnerships, funding, and community programs.

Founded in 2010, Green Initiative has been at the forefront of environmental advocacy, partnering with global organizations to drive change.

Vision

A world where sustainability is the norm.

Mission

To promote sustainable development and climate action through partnerships, funding, and community programs.
